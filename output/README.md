This directory contains all figures produced by my jupyter notebooks.

Figure 1:
This figure is a line chart that looks at informal employment rates across South Africa and Brazil +/- 2 years from their respective tournaments. It is aggregated across all hosts and all non hosts, where the dotted lines indicate non-host group. It has a total of 4 lines, with two for each country (host, non host).

Figure 2:
This figure disaggregates specific host and non host cities. It then looks at the change in informal employment rate from two years before and two years after the respective tournament. Each bar represents a different city, and its hosting status is flagged on the figure.

Figure 3:
This figure looks specifically at Johannesburg as it was the largest host metro for the South African cup. It then disaggregates by gender to see the informal rate compared between men and women across four years.

Figure 4:
This figure stays in Johannesburg, and looks only at men to try and disentangle the increase seen in Figure 3. It seperates individuals out by three sectors (construction, retail, and community work), and looks at each sector's change across the four years.
